Seasoned ground beef patties cook without skewers and can be tucked into warm pita with cucumber and
yogurt sauce.

## Ingredients

### Patties

- 2 lbs lean ground beef
- 2 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp salt
- Black pepper, to taste
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley, or 2 tbsp dried parsley
- 2 tbsp olive oil, for the pan

### To serve

- Greek-style pita bread
- Prepared tzatziki
- 1 cucumber, chopped
- Pickled red onions or fresh red onion
- Crumbled feta
- Additional fresh parsley

## Instructions

1. **Season and shape.** Distribute the spices and parsley through the beef without working it into
   a paste. Divide into 8 patties of even thickness, or form the recorded alternative of 16 small
   meatballs.
2. **Cook with space around each piece.** Heat some of the measured oil in a heavy skillet over
   medium-high heat. Add an uncrowded batch. Patties generally need 4-5 minutes on each side, but
   thickness matters more than the clock. Turn meatballs as needed to brown their surfaces.
3. **Check the center.** Cook ground beef or lamb to 160°F; a turkey substitution needs 165°F. Rest
   the cooked pieces for 2-3 minutes on a clean plate while finishing the remaining batches with the
   rest of the oil.
4. **Build the pitas.** Warm bread in a damp towel in the microwave for about 30 seconds. Add
   tzatziki, patties, cucumber, onions, feta and fresh parsley.

## Notes

- Ground lamb, ground turkey or equal amounts of beef and lamb are the recorded alternatives. Turkey
  has a higher minimum cooking temperature.
- Premix cumin, coriander, paprika and garlic powder for convenience. Store dry in a tightly closed
  container, using about 6 months as a flavor-quality window rather than a safety guarantee.
- Dairy is in the serving sauces and cheese; ordinary pita contains wheat. Check purchased sauces
  and bread labels.
- Keep raw or uncooked meat, its juices and utensils away from ready-to-eat ingredients. Wash hands
  and clean contact surfaces.
  [Safe cooking temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures).

## Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in shallow covered containers at 40°F or below within 2 hours of cooking or
preparation, or within 1 hour when the surroundings exceed 90°F. Use within 3-4 days. Reheat to
165°F throughout, adding a little water or the recipe liquid if needed; use a thermometer rather
than time alone. Freeze cooked patties separately from bread, vegetables and sauce; thaw in the
refrigerator.

[USDA cooling, storage and reheating guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

A stirred New Orleans cocktail combining rye, cognac, sweet vermouth, Bénédictine and two bitters.

## Ingredients

- 3/4 fl oz rye whiskey
- 3/4 fl oz cognac
- 3/4 fl oz sweet vermouth
- 1 barspoon Bénédictine, recorded as about 1/8 fl oz
- 2 dashes Peychaud's bitters
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- Ice for mixing and serving
- A lemon twist and/or cocktail cherry for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Measure the drink.** Put the rye, cognac, vermouth, Bénédictine and both bitters in a mixing
   glass.
2. **Chill by stirring.** Add ice and stir for about 30 seconds, until the glass feels cold and the
   drink has diluted slightly.
3. **Strain and finish.** Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice, using a large cube if available.
   Add the lemon twist, cherry or both.

## Notes

- Barspoons vary in capacity. Use the recorded approximate 1/8 fl oz measure when repeatability
  matters rather than assuming every barspoon is identical.
- Stirring, rather than shaking, keeps this spirit-based drink relatively clear.
- This is an alcoholic drink for adults of legal drinking age. Do not drive after drinking.
- Check vermouth, bitters and garnish labels for allergens or sulfites.

## Storage

Mix for immediate serving rather than storing a diluted drink. Refrigerate opened sweet vermouth and
follow its maker's guidance; store spirits and bitters according to their labels. Use clean ice and
fresh or properly stored garnish.

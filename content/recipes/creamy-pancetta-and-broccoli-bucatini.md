Cream cheese and a little cream bind the pasta, broccoli and a generous amount of sun-dried
tomatoes.

## Ingredients

- 250g bucatini, or up to 300g
- Water and salt for boiling
- 1 head broccoli, divided into florets
- 1/2 tbsp butter
- 1/2 tbsp olive oil
- 150g pancetta, diced
- 1 brown onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, chopped
- 270g sun-dried tomatoes
- 2 tbsp cream cheese
- 150ml heavy cream
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- Parmesan, grated for the sauce and shaved for serving
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 3/4 tsp chili flakes
- Black pepper, to taste
- Fresh parsley, optional garnish

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the tomato and broccoli.** If the tomatoes are dry-packed, soften them according to
   their package; drain oil-packed tomatoes. Chop as needed. Bring a large pot of salted water to a
   boil and cook the broccoli for 2 minutes. Lift it out, keeping the water for the pasta.
2. **Render the pancetta.** Heat the butter and oil in a wide skillet over medium heat. Cook the
   diced pancetta, stirring occasionally, until browned and fully cooked according to its package.
   Transfer to a dish and leave about 2 tbsp fat in the pan.
3. **Build a concentrated base.** Cook the onion with the garlic powder, onion powder, paprika and
   chili flakes until softened. Stir in the tomatoes for 2 minutes, then the fresh garlic just until
   fragrant.
4. **Cook the pasta and finish the sauce together.** Boil the bucatini to the package's al dente
   point, stirring well during the first 30-60 seconds. Meanwhile, melt the cream cheese into the
   skillet with the cream and honey or maple syrup. Add the broccoli and half the pancetta.
5. **Adjust the consistency.** Before draining the pasta, save some cooking water. Toss the drained
   bucatini with the sauce, adding small splashes of that water and grated Parmesan until it coats
   the strands. Taste before adding more salt or pepper. Serve with the remaining pancetta, shaved
   Parmesan and optional parsley.

## Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in shallow covered containers at 40°F or below within 2 hours of cooking or
preparation, or within 1 hour when the surroundings exceed 90°F. Use within 3-4 days. Reheat to
165°F throughout, adding a little water or the recipe liquid if needed; use a thermometer rather
than time alone. Refrigerated leftovers retain a better cream-sauce texture than frozen ones.

[USDA cooling, storage and reheating guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

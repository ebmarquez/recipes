Crackly crust, flaky salt on top, dramatic ear. Built around a 475°F home oven and a Dutch oven.
Makes one boule.

## Ingredients

- **100g** active starter (100% hydration; reliably rises after feeding)
- **340g** water, room temp _(68% added water relative to added flour; about 71% overall with 100%
  hydration starter)_
- **500g** bread flour _(or 450g bread + 50g whole wheat for more flavor)_
- **10g** salt
- **Maldon / flaky salt** — for the top
- Extra flour for dusting
- Extra water for wetting hands and spritzing

## Timeline (overnight method)

Mix → bulk ferment → shape → cold proof for 12–16 hours → bake the following day. Choose a start
time that allows the full fermentation and proof; a late-evening mix will not necessarily be ready
to bake the next morning.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mix, ferment and proof

Follow the dough cues as well as the clock.

1. **Mix and rest:** Stir starter into the 340g water until milky. Add flour, mix to a shaggy dough.
   Rest 30–60 min.
2. **Add salt:** Sprinkle salt on, pinch and squeeze through the dough until fully worked in.
3. **Bulk ferment (~4–6 hrs, room temp):** For the first 2–3 hrs, do a **stretch-and-fold** every
   30–45 min (wet hand, stretch one side up and over, rotate bowl, 4x per set). Dough goes shaggy →
   smooth/elastic. Done when risen ~50%, domed, and jiggly.
4. **Shape:** Turn onto lightly floured counter, shape into a taut ball. Place seam-side up in a
   floured towel-lined bowl or banneton.
5. **Cold proof (12–16 hrs, fridge):** Builds flavor, firms the dough for clean scoring and a better
   ear.

### Step 2: Bake in a 475°F oven

Handle the hot pot and lid with oven mitts.

1. **Preheat an oven-safe Dutch oven at 475°F for a full 60 min.** Check the pot, lid knob and
   parchment temperature ratings.
2. Flip cold dough onto parchment, **dust with flour**.
3. **Score** one deep slash at a shallow **~30° angle** (blade nearly sideways under the surface) —
   this makes the ear.
4. **Spritz the top with water**, sprinkle **Maldon flakes** (water helps them stick).
5. Into the Dutch oven, **475°F, lid on for 20 min**.
6. **Lid off, drop to 450°F, bake 25–30 min** until deep mahogany. Watch color, not clock.
7. **Cool on a rack 1 hr+** before cutting. Uncovered, or crust softens.

## Crust & Look Tips

- **Steam = crust.** The Dutch oven steams the loaf (lid on), then crisps it (lid off). Biggest
  single factor.
- **Bake dark** — almost mahogany. Pale = soft crust.
- Pale even when dark on top? Leave in the turned-off oven, door cracked, 5–10 min to dry the crust.
- **Flaky salt only** — table salt dissolves and over-salts.
- Light flour dusting before scoring gives rustic pale/dark contrast.

## Hydration Notes

These percentages describe added water divided by the 500g added flour; they exclude starter. With
100g of 100% hydration starter, the overall hydrations are about 68%, 71%, and 77%, respectively.

- **65%** (325g water) — easiest, tight ball, nearly foolproof
- **68%** (340g water) — _this recipe_, forgiving + open crumb
- **75%** (375g water) — wet, big airy holes, demands good handling

## Storage and Flour Safety

Cool completely before storing. Keep at room temperature for 2-3 days or slice and freeze for longer
storage. Do not taste raw dough; flour is a raw ingredient.

[Raw dough guidance](https://www.foodsafety.gov/blog/say-no-raw-dough).

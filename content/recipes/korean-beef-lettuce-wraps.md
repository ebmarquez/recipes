Sweet, savory, spicy ground beef in crisp butter lettuce cups. One skillet, 20 minutes, zero
leftovers because everyone inhales them.

**Prep:** 5 minutes | **Cook:** 15 minutes | **Total:** 20 minutes | **Yield:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs ground beef (80/20)
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated (or ½ tsp ground ginger)
- 2 heads butter lettuce (or Bibb lettuce)
- 4-5 green onions, sliced
- Sesame seeds for garnish
- Steamed rice for serving
- Sriracha for an optional finishing drizzle

**Substitutions:** Ground turkey or pork for the beef. Coconut aminos for soy sauce (gluten-free).
Sriracha + 1 tsp brown sugar if no gochujang. Honey instead of brown sugar.

## Instructions

1. **Mix the sauce** — Whisk soy sauce, gochujang, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and brown sugar in a
   small bowl. Set aside.
2. **Cook the beef (8 min)** — Large skillet, medium-high heat. Add ground beef, break it up. Cook
   until browned and at least 160°F internally (165°F for ground turkey). Drain excess fat if
   needed, leave about 1 tbsp in the pan.
3. **Build the flavor (3 min)** — Add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the
   sauce. Stir and let it simmer 2-3 min until it thickens and coats the beef.
4. **Assemble** — Spoon beef into lettuce cups. Top with sliced green onions, sesame seeds, and a
   drizzle of sriracha if you want more heat. Serve with steamed rice on the side.

## Chef's Tips

- **Gochujang is the move** — fermented chili paste with depth. Find it in the Asian aisle. Once you
  buy it, you'll use it on everything.
- **Reheats great** — the beef keeps 4 days in the fridge. Reheat in a skillet, add a splash of
  water. Lettuce stays separate until serving.
- **Make it a bowl** — skip the lettuce, serve over rice with a fried egg, pickled cucumber, and
  kimchi.

## Variations

- **Spicier** → add a diced Thai chili or more gochujang
- **Add crunch** → top with shredded carrots, sliced radish, or crushed peanuts
- **Pork version** → ground pork + hoisin sauce instead of gochujang

## Dietary Notes

Ordinary soy sauce and some gochujang contain wheat. Check both, plus hoisin or other substitutions.
Coconut aminos alone does not make the whole dish gluten-free. Check ingredient labels and
cross-contact information for allergies.

## Cooking Safety

Cook ground beef or pork to 160°F; ground turkey to 165°F. Keep raw meat and its utensils separate
from ready-to-eat foods; wash hands and clean work surfaces after handling raw meat.

[Safe cooking temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures).

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling boil
when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

[USDA leftover handling guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

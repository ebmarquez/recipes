Cook the seasoned thighs in the air fryer and make a separate garlic-maple glaze on the stove.

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- Zest of 1 lemon
- ⅛ cup soy sauce (2 tbsp)
- ¼ cup maple syrup
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika

### Glaze and serving

- 2 tbsp butter
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ cup maple syrup
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 3 tsp Momofuku chili oil, or another chili oil or chili crisp
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp water
- Sliced green onions or sesame seeds, optional
- Cooked rice and vegetables, optional

## Instructions

1. **Season and preheat.** Toss the thighs with the lemon zest, the 2 tbsp soy sauce, ¼ cup maple
   syrup, garlic powder and paprika. Preheat the air fryer to 400°F. Keep raw-chicken utensils
   separate from the glaze ingredients.
2. **Air-fry without stacking.** Spread a batch of thighs in one layer. Turn halfway through a
   roughly 15-minute cooking period. Start checking near 12 minutes and continue until each thickest
   portion reaches at least 165°F. Repeat for any remaining chicken.
3. **Prepare the glaze while the chicken cooks.** Stir the cornstarch and water together in a cup.
   In a saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat and stir in the garlic for about 30 seconds. Add
   the glaze's ½ cup maple syrup, ⅓ cup soy sauce, vinegar and chili oil. Simmer for 3-4 minutes.
4. **Thicken and coat.** Stir the slurry again, whisk it into the simmering glaze and cook until it
   lightly coats a spoon. Toss in the cooked chicken using clean utensils. Discard any marinade left
   in the raw-chicken bowl; it is not part of the finishing glaze.
5. **Serve.** Add the optional green onions or sesame seeds. Spoon extra glaze over rice and
   vegetables if using.

## Notes

- [Steamed Jasmine Rice](../steamed-jasmine-rice/) and
  [Sesame-Garlic Broccoli](../sesame-garlic-broccoli/) are existing accompaniments; their cooking
  time is separate.
- Honey can replace maple syrup, with a different flavor and browning behavior. Reduce or omit chili
  oil for a milder version.
- For gluten-free preparation, use verified gluten-free tamari and check the chili oil/crisp, not
  just the soy sauce. Butter adds milk; sesame garnish and some chili oils add sesame.

- Keep raw or uncooked meat, its juices and utensils away from ready-to-eat ingredients. Wash hands

## Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in shallow covered containers at 40°F or below within 2 hours of cooking or
preparation, or within 1 hour when the surroundings exceed 90°F. Use within 4 days. Reheat to 165°F
throughout, adding a little water or the recipe liquid if needed; use a thermometer rather than time
alone. Freeze cooked chicken with glaze in meal-sized portions; thaw in the refrigerator before
reheating.

[USDA cooling, storage and reheating guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

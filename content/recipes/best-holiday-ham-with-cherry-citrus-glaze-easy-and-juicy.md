Warm a fully cooked spiral ham over apple juice, then add a cherry, orange and mustard glaze near
the end.

## Ingredients

### Ham and roasting liquid

- 1 fully cooked spiral-sliced ham, weight unspecified in the saved recipe
- BBQ seasoning, enough for the outside of the ham
- 1 orange, sliced
- 2-3 cups apple juice
- 3-4 cinnamon sticks
- Whole cloves, optional

### Glaze

- 1/2 cup cherry preserves
- 1 1/2 cups brown sugar
- 1/4 cup Dijon mustard
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 US stick butter (8 tbsp)
- Juice and zest of 1 orange
- A pinch of ground cinnamon
- A pinch of ground clove or allspice
- A dash of Frank's RedHot or similar hot sauce

## Instructions

1. **Check the ham label.** This method is for fully cooked ham, not fresh or cook-before-eating
   ham. Note the package's warming directions and use a thermometer; an unspecified ham weight makes
   a fixed total time unreliable. Heat the oven to 325°F.
2. **Prepare the roaster.** Set a rack in a roasting pan. Put the apple juice, sliced orange,
   cinnamon sticks and optional whole cloves below it. Unwrap the ham, apply BBQ seasoning and place
   it cut-side down on the rack. Cover with foil.
3. **Warm gradually.** Bake covered, beginning checks during the saved recipe's 1½-2-hour window.
   Keep warming until a ham packaged in a USDA-inspected plant reaches 140°F, or use 165°F for a
   repackaged or leftover cooked ham. Follow any higher package requirement. Add time rather than
   relying on a 120-130°F intermediate reading.
4. **Cook the glaze separately.** Combine all glaze ingredients in a small saucepan. Stir over
   moderate heat until the butter melts, then simmer for 6-8 minutes, stirring to prevent scorching.
5. **Finish the surface.** Reserve a clean portion of glaze for serving. Uncover the ham, brush on
   the remaining glaze and increase the oven to 400°F. Allow roughly 15-25 minutes for the coating
   to darken, brushing again halfway through and watching closely. If the sugar darkens too quickly,
   shield it with foil.
6. **Carve.** Rest for 10-15 minutes before separating slices. Serve with the reserved glaze, using
   a clean brush or spoon.

## Notes

- The original yield is 10-12 servings, but no ham weight was supplied; treat that yield as an
  estimate when shopping.
- Apricot preserves, peach preserves or cranberry sauce can replace the cherry preserves. Their
  sugar content will affect browning.
- Make the glaze 2-3 days in advance and refrigerate it separately.
- Butter contains milk. Check the ham cure, BBQ blend, mustard and preserves before describing the
  complete meal as gluten-free.
- The ham-specific temperature targets follow the
  [safe cooking temperature chart](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures).

## Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in shallow covered containers at 40°F or below within 2 hours of cooking or
preparation, or within 1 hour when the surroundings exceed 90°F. Use within 3-4 days. Reheat to
165°F throughout, adding a little water or the recipe liquid if needed; use a thermometer rather
than time alone. Freeze sliced portions with a little cooking liquid for later meals.

[USDA cooling, storage and reheating guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

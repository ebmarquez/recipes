## Overview

Total weeknight comfort food. Ground beef and egg noodles come together in a single skillet — the
noodles cook right in the savory broth so there's no separate pasta pot to wash. Ready in about 30
minutes, easily doubled, and reheats well for leftovers.

## Ingredients

### For the Skillet

- 1.5 lbs ground beef (80/20)
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1 tsp paprika
- Salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste
- 8 oz wide egg noodles

### To Finish

- 1/2 cup sour cream (or 1 cup shredded cheddar for a cheesy version)
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Instructions

### Step 1: Brown the Beef

Heat a large deep skillet over medium-high heat

- Add ground beef, onion, and garlic
- Cook 6-8 minutes, breaking up the meat, until browned and the onion softens
- Drain excess fat

### Step 2: Build the Sauce

Stir in tomato paste, Worcestershire, paprika, salt, and pepper

- Cook 1 minute until the tomato paste darkens slightly (this deepens the flavor)
- Pour in the beef broth and scrape up any browned bits

### Step 3: Cook the Noodles In the Pan

Add the egg noodles right into the skillet, pushing them down into the liquid

- Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook 10-12 minutes, stirring once or twice, until noodles are tender
  and the liquid has thickened into a sauce
- _If it looks dry before the noodles are done, splash in a little more broth or water_

### Step 4: Finish

Remove from heat

- Swirl in the sour cream until creamy (or sprinkle in cheddar and stir until melted)
- Taste and adjust salt/pepper
- Garnish with parsley and serve immediately

## Variations & Substitutions

- **No sour cream?** Use cream cheese, plain Greek yogurt, or go cheesy with cheddar
- **No Worcestershire?** Soy sauce + a pinch of sugar works
- **Add veggies:** Stir in a handful of frozen peas or sliced mushrooms with the broth
- **Tex-Mex twist:** Swap paprika for taco seasoning, finish with cheddar + a spoon of salsa
- **Italian twist:** Swap broth for marinara + a splash of water, finish with parmesan

## Timing

- **Prep:** 10 minutes

## Make-Ahead & Storage

- Stores well in the fridge 3-4 days
- Reheat with a splash of broth or water to loosen the sauce
- Add sour cream fresh when reheating if you want it creamiest

## Cooking Safety

Cook ground beef to 160°F, not merely until its color changes. Keep raw meat and its utensils
separate from ready-to-eat foods; wash hands and clean work surfaces after handling raw meat.

[Safe cooking temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures).

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 3-4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling
boil when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

[USDA leftover handling guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

## Dietary Notes

Egg noodles contain eggs and wheat; Worcestershire may contain fish, and soy-sauce substitutions add
soy. Check ingredient labels and cross-contact information for allergies.

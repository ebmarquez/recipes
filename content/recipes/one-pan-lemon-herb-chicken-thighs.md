Crispy skin-on chicken thighs with olives, artichoke hearts, capers, and a bright lemon-herb sauce.
Mediterranean vibes, one pan, minimal effort — maximum flavor.

**Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** About 23-26 minutes, plus any extra batches | **Total:** About
38-41 minutes including 5-minute rest; batches add time | **Yield:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 6-8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 1 can (14oz) artichoke hearts, drained and quartered
- ½ cup Kalamata olives, halved
- 2 tbsp capers, drained
- 4 garlic cloves, smashed
- 2 lemons (1 juiced, 1 sliced into rounds)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp dried oregano (or Italian seasoning)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Fresh parsley or basil for finishing
- Kosher salt & black pepper
- Optional: ½ cup cherry tomatoes

**Substitutions:** Boneless skinless thighs (begin checking about 5 minutes earlier; still cook to
165°F). Green olives instead of Kalamata. Sun-dried tomatoes instead of artichokes.

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven** to 425°F.
2. **Season the chicken** — Pat thighs dry with paper towels (crucial for crispy skin). Season
   generously with salt, pepper, oregano, and smoked paprika on both sides.
3. **Sear the chicken (6 min)** — Large oven-safe skillet, medium-high heat, 2 tbsp olive oil. Place
   thighs skin-side down. Sear 5-6 min until the skin is golden and crispy. Don't move them — let
   the skin do its thing. Flip once.
4. **Add the good stuff** — Scatter artichoke hearts, olives, capers, garlic, cherry tomatoes (if
   using), and lemon slices around the chicken. Squeeze lemon juice over everything.
5. **Roast (18-20 min)** — Transfer skillet to oven. Roast until chicken reaches 165°F internal and
   the skin is deeply golden.
6. **Finish** — Rest 5 min. Garnish with fresh parsley or basil. Serve straight from the skillet.

## Chef's Tips

- **Dry the skin** — this is non-negotiable. Wet skin = no crisp. Pat it dry, then season.
- **Don't crowd the pan** — if your skillet is small, work in batches or use two pans.
- **Serve with:** crusty bread to soak up the pan juices, couscous, orzo, or roasted potatoes.
- **Reheating:** Oven at 350°F until the center reaches 165°F; 10 minutes may not be enough. Skin
  won't be as crispy but the flavor holds up perfectly.

## Variations

- **Tuscan spin** → swap olives for sun-dried tomatoes, add white beans
- **Spicy** → add red pepper flakes or Calabrian chilis
- **Greens** → add a handful of spinach or kale in the last 5 min of roasting

## Dietary Notes

There is no dairy in the base recipe. Bread, couscous and orzo serving suggestions usually contain
wheat. Check ingredient labels and cross-contact information for allergies.

## Cooking Safety

Measure 165°F in the thickest part of each thigh, away from the bone. Keep raw meat and its utensils
separate from ready-to-eat foods; wash hands and clean work surfaces after handling raw meat.

[Safe cooking temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures).

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 3-4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling
boil when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

[USDA leftover handling guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

East Texas–style BBQ sauce — sweet, sticky, and smoky with a deep, oaky backbone from the whiskey.
Simmering blends the whiskey with the molasses and brown sugar; the sauce retains an
alcohol-containing ingredient. A crowd-pleasing sauce that's perfect slathered on brisket.

---

## Ingredients

### **Aromatics**

- 1 tbsp butter or oil
- ½ yellow onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced

### **Base**

- ⅓ cup whiskey or bourbon
- 1½ cups ketchup
- ⅓ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
- 3 tbsp molasses
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard

### **Seasoning**

- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp salt

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sweat the Aromatics

Melt the butter or warm the oil in a saucepan over medium heat.

- Add onion and cook ~4 minutes until soft and translucent.
- Add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.

### Step 2: Deglaze with Whiskey

**Pull the pan off the heat** and add the whiskey (it can flare if added over an open flame).

- Return to heat and simmer 2–3 minutes to mellow the flavor; this does not make the sauce
  alcohol-free.

### Step 3: Build the Sauce

Add ketchup, brown sugar, vinegar, molasses, Worcestershire, Dijon, smoked paprika, pepper, and
salt.

- Stir to combine.

### Step 4: Simmer

Simmer 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.

- For a silky-smooth sauce, blend with an immersion or countertop blender.

### Step 5: Finish

Taste and adjust — more brown sugar for sweet, more vinegar for tang.

- Sauce thickens as it cools, so pull it a touch thinner than your target.

---

## Notes

- **Makes ~2.5 cups.**
- Keep refrigerated for 3-4 days or freeze portions for longer storage; this is not a shelf-stable
  sauce.
- Pairs beautifully as a contrast to a spicier sauce like the 🌶️ Chipotle BBQ Sauce.
- For brisket: serve on the side or brush on during the last 20–30 minutes of cooking / resting.

## Serving and Heat

**Heat:** Mild.

**Suggested pairings:** Brisket, Pulled Pork, Ribs, Burgers.

## Dietary Notes

Standard Worcestershire often contains fish. Butter adds dairy; use oil if avoiding dairy. Whiskey
remains an alcoholic ingredient after simmering. Check ingredient labels and cross-contact
information for allergies.

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 3-4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling
boil when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

[USDA leftover handling guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

## Blending Caution

For a countertop blender, let hot sauce cool to the temperature allowed by the manufacturer; never
seal hot liquid in a personal blender. Blend in small batches with the lid vented as directed. An
immersion blender can avoid transferring the hot sauce; keep its blade submerged.

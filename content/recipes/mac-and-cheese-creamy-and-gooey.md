Fixed for maximum creaminess and stretch — the common "dry, not gooey" version fails from too little
dairy, no emulsifying cheese, and adding cheese while the sauce is boiling. This version corrects
all three.

**Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** 25 minutes | **Total:** 35 minutes | **Yield:** 6-8 servings

## Ingredients

### Pasta

- 1 lb elbow macaroni or cavatappi

### Cheese Sauce

- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 3 cups whole milk, warmed
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 cups sharp cheddar, shredded (from a block — not pre-shredded)
- 1 cup Gruyère or Monterey Jack, shredded
- 5 oz American cheese, torn into pieces (the "gooey" secret — emulsifies the sauce)
- ½ tsp dry mustard
- Pinch of nutmeg
- Salt & black pepper, to taste

### Topping (optional)

- ½ cup panko breadcrumbs
- 2 tbsp butter, melted

## Instructions

1. **Cook the pasta 2 minutes under al dente** in well-salted water. Drain and reserve ½ cup of the
   pasta cooking water. (Slightly firm pasta finishes in the sauce and won't turn mushy.)
2. **Make the roux:** Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook 1
   minute until it smells nutty.
3. **Build the béchamel:** Slowly whisk in the warm milk and cream. Simmer, whisking, until it
   thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, about 3–4 minutes. Stir in dry mustard and nutmeg.
4. **Add cheese OFF the heat:** Kill the burner and wait 30 seconds. Stir in all the cheese a
   handful at a time until glossy and smooth. If it tightens up, loosen with a splash of the
   reserved pasta water. (Boiling cheese seizes and goes grainy — off-heat keeps it silky.)
5. **Combine:** Fold in the drained pasta. The sauce should look _too loose_ at this stage — that's
   correct. It tightens as it sits. Season with salt and pepper.
6. **Serve or broil:** Serve immediately for maximum goo. OR transfer to a baking dish, top with
   panko tossed in melted butter, and **broil 2–3 minutes only** until golden. Do not bake long —
   extended baking is what dries mac & cheese out.

## Notes

- **The three changes that matter most:** more dairy (3 cups milk + 1 cup cream), American cheese
  for stretch, and adding cheese off the heat.
- **Whole milk only** — 2% or skim will make it thin and less creamy.
- **Shred from a block.** Pre-shredded bagged cheese has anti-caking starch that keeps it from
  melting smooth.
- No American cheese? Sodium citrate can help emulsify the sauce, but it is not a pinch-for-pinch or
  flavor-equivalent replacement; use a measured formulation from the product maker.
- Leftovers reheat best with a splash of milk stirred in over low heat.

## Dietary Notes

For a vegetarian version, check all cheeses for animal rennet. Pasta, flour and panko contain wheat.
Check ingredient labels and cross-contact information for allergies.

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 3-4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling
boil when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

[USDA leftover handling guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

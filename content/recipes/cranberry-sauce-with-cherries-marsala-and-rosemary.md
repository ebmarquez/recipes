Marsala-reduced dried cherries, frozen dark cherries, and fresh cranberries in a warmly-spiced
holiday sauce. Make it 3-4 days ahead and keep refrigerated.

**Prep:** 5 minutes | **Cook:** 20 minutes | **Total:** 3 hours 25 minutes (including chill) |
**Yield:** 3 cups

## Ingredients

- 2 cups Marsala wine
- 1/2 cup dried tart cherries
- 1 bag (12 oz) fresh cranberries
- 12 oz frozen dark cherries (about 2 2/3 cups), halved
- 1 cup packed golden brown sugar
- 1 tsp minced fresh rosemary
- 1/2 tsp ground allspice

## Instructions

1. **Reduce the Marsala:** Combine the Marsala and dried cherries in a deep saucepan. Boil until the
   mixture is reduced to about 2/3 cup, approximately 8 minutes.
2. **Cook the sauce:** Add the cranberries, frozen cherries, brown sugar, rosemary, and allspice.
   Bring to a boil, stirring occasionally.
3. **Simmer:** Reduce heat to medium, cover, and simmer until the cranberries burst and the mixture
   thickens, stirring occasionally, about 8 minutes.
4. **Chill:** Divide into shallow heat-safe containers and refrigerate promptly until cold, about 3
   hours.

## Notes

- Can be prepared 3-4 days ahead. Cover and keep refrigerated.
- The Marsala adds a rich, wine-forward depth — do not substitute with a sweet cooking wine.
- Serve alongside roast turkey, ham, or pork.

## Dietary Notes

For strict vegan diets, check the wine producer. Marsala is an alcoholic ingredient; reduction does
not establish an alcohol-free sauce. Check ingredient labels and cross-contact information for
allergies.

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 3-4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling
boil when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

[USDA leftover handling guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

Baby potatoes get crisp edges in the air fryer, then a coating of melted butter
and dill. Serve with Swedish meatballs and gravy or another saucy main.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb baby potatoes, washed and thoroughly dried
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt or 1/4 tsp fine salt, plus more to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh dill or 1 tsp dried dill

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the air fryer and potatoes.** Preheat the air fryer to 400 F if the
   manufacturer requires it. Halve or quarter the potatoes into roughly 1-inch
   pieces. Pat the cut surfaces dry.
2. **Season.** Toss the potatoes with the olive oil, measured salt, and pepper.
   Spread in the basket in a mostly single layer, leaving room for air to
   circulate. Do not pack the basket tightly.
3. **Air-fry.** Cook at 400 F for 18-22 minutes, shaking the basket halfway
   through. Start checking at 16 minutes. The potatoes are ready when browned
   and a fork slides easily into the centers; add time if needed for tenderness.
4. **Finish off the heat.** Transfer the hot potatoes to a bowl and toss with
   the melted butter and dill. Taste before adding more salt, then serve.

## Notes

- Air-fryer capacity and potato size affect timing. The 25-30 minute estimate
  assumes one uncrowded basket batch, including brief preheating. If the potatoes
  do not fit, cook in batches and allow extra time, or reduce the recipe.
- Add butter and dill only after cooking so they do not burn.
- Larger potatoes cut into the same roughly 1-inch pieces can replace baby
  potatoes; check the centers rather than relying on time alone.
- Pair with [Quick Cucumber-Dill Salad with Pickled Red Onion](

## Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in a shallow covered container at 40 F or below within
2 hours, or within 1 hour when the surroundings exceed 90 F. Use within
3-4 days. Reheat in the air fryer at 350 F, checking frequently, until the
potatoes reach 165 F throughout. The dill may darken during reheating.
Freezing is possible, but the potatoes may lose their crisp texture.

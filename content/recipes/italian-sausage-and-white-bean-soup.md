Rustic Tuscan one-pot soup. Browned sausage, creamy beans, wilted greens, Parmesan broth. A hearty
soup with a short simmer and plenty of texture.

**Prep:** 5 minutes | **Cook:** About 22 minutes, plus bringing to a boil | **Total:** About 27
minutes or longer | **Yield:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 lb Italian sausage (sweet or hot — casings removed)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning (or dried oregano + basil)
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 cans (15 oz each) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 4 cups fresh baby spinach (or kale, chopped)
- Parmesan rind (optional — drop in while simmering for umami depth)
- Grated Parmesan for serving
- Crusty bread for serving
- Salt and pepper, to taste

**Substitutions:** Turkey sausage for lighter version. Great Northern beans or chickpeas instead of
cannellini. Kale or escarole for spinach. Swap broth for bone broth for richer flavor.

## Instructions

1. **Brown the sausage (5 min)** — Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high. Add
   sausage, break into crumbles. Cook until browned and at least 160°F internally (165°F for turkey
   sausage). Remove to a plate if your pot is small, or just push to one side.
2. **Build the base (3 min)** — Add onion to the pot (use the sausage fat). Cook 2-3 min until
   softened. Add garlic, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes — cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. **Simmer (12 min)** — Return any reserved sausage to the pot. Add diced tomatoes, beans, and
   chicken broth. Drop in a Parmesan rind if you have one. Bring to a boil, reduce to medium-low,
   simmer 10-12 min. Mash a few beans against the side of the pot to thicken.
4. **Finish with greens (2 min)** — Stir in spinach, let it wilt 1-2 min. Remove and discard the
   Parmesan rind, if used. Season with salt and pepper.
5. **Serve** — Ladle into bowls, top with grated Parmesan, serve with crusty bread for dunking.

## Chef's Tips

- **Mash some beans** — crushing 8-10 beans against the pot gives you a creamy broth without adding
  cream. Old Italian trick.
- **Parmesan rind** — save those rinds in a freezer bag. They're liquid gold in soups and stews.
- **Even better day two** — the beans absorb the broth overnight. Add a splash of broth when
  reheating.
- **Freezes well** — 3 months in the freezer. Portion into containers.

## Dietary Notes

Parmesan contains milk. Check sausage and broth; ordinary crusty bread contains wheat. Check
ingredient labels and cross-contact information for allergies.

## Cooking Safety

Pork/beef sausage needs 160°F; turkey sausage needs 165°F. Keep raw meat and its utensils separate
from ready-to-eat foods; wash hands and clean work surfaces after handling raw meat.

[Safe cooking temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures).

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 3-4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling
boil when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

[USDA leftover handling guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

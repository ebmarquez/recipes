Two small chocolate cakes use the saved butter-and-chocolate formula, with a fully baked center
rather than deliberately underdone batter.

## Ingredients

- Cooking spray for the ramekins
- Unsweetened cocoa powder for coating
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter (1/2 US stick)
- 2 oz bittersweet chocolate, chopped
- 1 large egg
- 1 large egg yolk
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- A pinch of salt
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- Fresh raspberries, to serve
- Confectioners' sugar, to finish

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the dishes.** Heat the oven to 425°F. Spray two oven-safe 6-ounce ramekins, coat their
   interiors with cocoa and shake out the excess. Set them on a baking sheet.
2. **Melt gently.** Put the chocolate and butter in a heatproof bowl above barely simmering water,
   without touching the water. Stir until smooth, then remove the bowl and let it cool until warm
   rather than hot.
3. **Make the batter.** Beat the egg, yolk, granulated sugar and salt with a hand mixer for about 2
   minutes, until thickened and pale. Mix in the warm chocolate at low speed. Sift in the flour and
   fold only until no dry pockets remain. Do not taste the batter.
4. **Bake through.** Divide between the ramekins. Begin checking at 10-12 minutes, but continue
   baking until the centers are set, not liquid batter, and a skewer shows moist crumbs rather than
   wet flour batter. Confirm the centers reach at least 160°F for the egg component; temperature
   alone does not replace checking that the flour batter is fully baked.
5. **Plate carefully.** Wait at least 1 minute, then loosen the edges. Use oven mitts to invert onto
   plates, or serve directly in the ramekins. Add raspberries and confectioners' sugar.

## Notes

- This adaptation intentionally does not promise the source's molten center. Keeping the original
  egg and flour amounts while baking them through produces a different texture.
- Filled ramekins may be covered and refrigerated for up to 2 hours before baking. Cold batter takes
  longer; do not assume an additional 1-2 minutes guarantees completion.
- Do not home-heat-treat flour as a shortcut for serving raw batter. See
  [FDA flour guidance](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/flour-raw-food-and-other-safety-facts)
  and
  [egg safety guidance](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety).
- Butter and chocolate can contain milk; eggs and wheat flour are also present. Check chocolate and
  spray labels for allergens.

## Storage

Refrigerate the fully baked cakes in covered containers at 40°F or below within 2 hours, or within 1
hour above 90°F. Use within 3-4 days. Serve chilled, or reheat portions to 165°F if serving warm.
Freeze without berries or sugar garnish and thaw in the refrigerator; add the garnishes when
serving.

[FDA cooked-egg storage guidance](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety).

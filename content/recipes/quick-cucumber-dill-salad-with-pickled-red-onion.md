Cool cucumber and tangy pickled onion balance a rich main without another pan
on the stove. A short rest lets the dill and vinegar season the cucumber.

## Ingredients

- 1 large English cucumber, washed and thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp prepared pickled red onion, drained and thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp white vinegar or apple cider vinegar, plus more to taste
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh dill or 1 tsp dried dill
- Black pepper, to taste
- Sugar, a pinch if needed after tasting
- Fine salt, a pinch if needed after tasting

## Instructions

1. **Dress the cucumber.** Combine the cucumber, drained pickled red onion,
   measured vinegar, dill, and pepper in a serving bowl. Toss well. Leave out
   the sugar and salt for now; the pickled onions already contribute seasoning.
2. **Rest.** Let stand for 10 minutes, then toss again.
3. **Adjust and serve.** Taste a cucumber slice with a little onion. Add more
   vinegar if it needs tang, a pinch of sugar if it is too sharp, or a pinch of
   salt if needed. Serve with a slotted spoon to leave excess dressing behind.

## Notes

- Use already-prepared, chilled pickled onions. Store-bought onions work, or
  use [Garlic-Pepper Refrigerator Red Onions](../easy-pickled-red-onions/)
  made ahead. Making and chilling a new batch is not included in the timing.
- Pickled onions vary in acidity, sweetness, and saltiness. Start with the
  listed tablespoon of vinegar instead of adding extra pickle brine.
- Fresh dill gives a brighter flavor; dried dill softens during the rest.
- Serve alongside [Air-Fryer Baby Potatoes with Butter and Dill](
  ../air-fryer-baby-potatoes-with-butter-and-dill/) and Swedish meatballs.

## Storage

Refrigerate in a covered container at 40 F or below within 2 hours of
preparation, or within 1 hour when the surroundings exceed 90 F. Best within
1-2 days because the cucumber softens and releases water. Do not extend the
pickled onions' original storage limit by mixing them into the salad.
Serve chilled; freezing is not recommended.

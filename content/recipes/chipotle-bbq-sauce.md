South Texas / Tex-Mex–style BBQ sauce — smoky, spicy, and deep, with bold heat from chipotles in
adobo and a bright finish of lime. A perfect contrast against a sweet, boozy sauce like the 🥃
Whiskey BBQ Sauce. Excellent on brisket and even better on leftover brisket tacos.

---

## Ingredients

### **Aromatics**

- 1 tbsp oil
- ½ yellow onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced

### **Base**

- 2–3 chipotle chiles in adobo + 1 tbsp adobo sauce _(start with 2 for medium heat)_
- 1½ cups ketchup _(or tomato sauce for less sweet)_
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
- 2 tbsp molasses or honey
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce

### **Seasoning**

- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp salt
- Juice of ½ lime _(stir in at the end)_

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Sweat the Aromatics

Heat oil in a saucepan over medium heat.

- Add onion and cook until soft, ~4 minutes.
- Add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.

### Step 2: Build the Sauce

Add chipotles + adobo sauce, ketchup (or tomato sauce), brown sugar, vinegar, molasses (or honey),
Worcestershire, cumin, smoked paprika, pepper, and salt.

- Stir to combine.

### Step 3: Simmer

Simmer 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.

### Step 4: Blend

**Blend smooth** with an immersion or countertop blender — this is important to break down the
chipotle chunks for a silky sauce.

### Step 5: Finish

Stir in the lime juice off the heat.

- Taste — add another chipotle if you want more burn.
- Sauce thickens as it cools, so pull it a touch thinner than your target.

---

## Notes

- **Makes ~2.5 cups.**
- Heat scales with the chipotles — 2 is medium, 3+ brings real burn.
- The adobo sauce is where the smoky magic lives — don't skip it.
- Keep refrigerated for 3-4 days or freeze portions for longer storage; this is not a shelf-stable
  sauce.
- For brisket: serve on the side, or brush on during the last 20–30 minutes. Outstanding on leftover
  brisket tacos with pickled onions.

## Serving and Heat

**Heat:** Medium-High.

**Suggested pairings:** Brisket, Pulled Pork, Tacos, Ribs.

## Dietary Notes

Worcestershire often contains fish and may contain gluten. Honey is not vegan; check all bottled
sauces for dietary restrictions. Check ingredient labels and cross-contact information for
allergies.

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 3-4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling
boil when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

[USDA leftover handling guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

## Blending Caution

For a countertop blender, let hot sauce cool to the temperature allowed by the manufacturer; never
seal hot liquid in a personal blender. Blend in small batches with the lid vented as directed. An
immersion blender can avoid transferring the hot sauce; keep its blade submerged.

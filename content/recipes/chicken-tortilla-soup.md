Smoky, bright, loaded Tex-Mex soup using a rotisserie chicken shortcut. Charred tortilla strips,
creamy avocado, lime crema. This is the soup that tricks people into thinking you cooked all day.

**Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** About 17 minutes, plus bringing to a boil | **Total:** About 27
minutes or longer; extra time for homemade tortilla strips | **Yield:** 4-6 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 store rotisserie chicken (shred ~3 cups meat)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced (leave seeds for heat)
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 can (14 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 4 cups chicken broth
- Juice of 2 limes
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Toppings

- Avocado, diced
- Sour cream or Mexican crema
- Tortilla strips or crushed tortilla chips
- Fresh cilantro
- Shredded cheese (Mexican blend or cotija)
- Lime wedges

**Substitutions:** Leftover chicken or canned chicken for the rotisserie. Pinto beans for black
beans. Regular diced tomatoes + pinch of liquid smoke if no fire-roasted. Greek yogurt for sour
cream.

## Instructions

1. **Shred the chicken** — Pull meat from rotisserie chicken while it's still warm. Discard skin and
   bones. Set aside ~3 cups shredded meat.
2. **Build the base (5 min)** — Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and
   jalapeño, cook 3-4 min until softened. Add garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and chili powder — cook
   30 seconds until fragrant and toasty.
3. **Simmer (10 min)** — Add fire-roasted tomatoes, black beans, and chicken broth. Bring to a boil,
   reduce heat, simmer 10 min.
4. **Add chicken and lime (2 min)** — Stir in shredded chicken and lime juice. Heat through to
   165°F, allowing more than 1-2 minutes if needed. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
5. **Load up the toppings** — Ladle into bowls. Top with avocado, crema, tortilla strips, cilantro,
   cheese, and a lime wedge.

## Chef's Tips

- **Rotisserie chicken is not cheating** — it's called being smart. The flavor is already built in
  from the seasoning and roasting.
- **Char your own tortilla strips** — cut corn tortillas into strips, toss with oil, bake at 400°F
  for 8-10 min. Way better than store-bought.
- **Fire-roasted tomatoes matter** — the smoky char they add is half the flavor. Don't sub regular
  tomatoes unless you have to.
- **Freezes great** — soup base freezes 3 months. Add fresh toppings after reheating.

## Dietary Notes

Use gluten-free broth, chicken seasoning and tortilla chips. Cheese and crema toppings contain milk.
Check ingredient labels and cross-contact information for allergies.

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 3-4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling
boil when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

[USDA leftover handling guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

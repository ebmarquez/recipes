A comforting and savory chicken miso ramen made in under 40 minutes. Chicken is simmered directly in a toasted miso aromatics broth for deep, rich flavor.

**Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** 25 minutes | **Total:** 35 minutes | **Yield:** 2 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil (or cooking oil)
- 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp miso paste (red, white, or mixed)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp red pepper paste (doubanjiang, sriracha, or chili paste for mild heat; optional)
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken stock
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs (approx. 3/4 to 1 lb)
- 2 servings ramen noodles
- 1 tbsp butter (optional, for finishing)

## Instructions

1. **Sauté Aromatics:** Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger, cooking for about 1 minute until fragrant.
2. **Build Miso Base:** Lower heat slightly and stir in the miso paste, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sugar, and red pepper paste (if using). Stir constantly for 1–2 minutes to gently toast the miso without burning it.
3. **Add Stock & Chicken:** Pour in the 4 cups of chicken stock and stir well to dissolve the paste. Place the raw chicken directly into the pot.
4. **Simmer & Shred:** Bring to a gentle simmer, then cover and cook on medium-low for 15–20 minutes until the chicken reaches 165°F. Remove the chicken, shred it using two forks, and return the shredded meat to the pot.
5. **Simmer & Serve:** Cook your ramen noodles separately according to package directions, then drain. Ladle the hot chicken miso broth and shredded chicken over the noodles in serving bowls. Add a knob of butter (about 1 tbsp) into the broth right before serving for a silky richness.

## Notes

- **Substitution:** Any high-heat cooking oil can be used if toasted sesame oil is not available. Swap chicken breast for boneless, skinless chicken thighs or sliced tofu. Use tamari and gluten-free ramen noodles to make this gluten-free.
- **Budget:** Chicken thighs are often more budget-friendly and remain extremely tender when simmered.
- **Food safety:** Always use a food thermometer to ensure the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F.

## Make-Ahead and Storage

- **Refrigerate:** Store the broth and shredded chicken together in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Always store cooked ramen noodles separately from the broth so they do not absorb all the liquid and become mushy.
- **Freeze:** The broth and chicken can be frozen together in a freezer-safe container for up to 3 months. Do not freeze the cooked noodles.
- **Reheat:** Reheat the broth and chicken on the stovetop over medium heat until it comes to a simmer (leftovers should reach 165°F), then pour over freshly prepared ramen noodles.

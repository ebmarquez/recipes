Give the carrots a head start in the oven before adding garlic and cheese so the topping browns
later.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb medium carrots, trimmed and halved lengthwise
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
- 2 garlic cloves, minced

## Instructions

1. **Heat the oven and season.** Set the oven to 425°F. Put the carrots on a rimmed baking sheet
   lined with parchment rated for that temperature. Coat with oil, salt and pepper, then spread them
   out.
2. **Roast in two stages.** Bake for 10 minutes. Turn the carrots and distribute the garlic and
   Parmesan over them. Return to the oven for about 10 minutes, until a fork enters the thickest
   pieces easily and the cheese has melted.
3. **Serve.** Lift the carrots off the tray with any browned cheese. If unusually thick carrots
   remain firm, allow a little more time and watch the garlic closely.

## Notes

- Choose similarly sized carrots for even cooking.
- Parmesan contains milk and may use animal rennet; select suitable cheese for a vegetarian diet.
- Source nutrition figures were not recalculated for this adaptation, so no nutrition numbers are

## Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in shallow covered containers at 40°F or below within 2 hours of cooking or
preparation, or within 1 hour when the surroundings exceed 90°F. Use within 3-4 days. Reheat to
165°F throughout, adding a little water or the recipe liquid if needed; use a thermometer rather
than time alone. Freezing is possible but produces softer carrots and a less crisp topping.

[USDA cooling, storage and reheating guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

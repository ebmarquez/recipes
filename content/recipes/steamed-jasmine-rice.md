Fluffy, fragrant stovetop jasmine rice that is ideal for soaking up sauces from stir-fries, glazed
meats, and curries.

**Prep:** 5 minutes | **Cook:** 15 minutes | **Total:** 30 minutes | **Yield:** 4-6 servings

## Ingredients

- 2 cups jasmine rice
- 2½ cups water
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp butter, optional
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced, optional

## Instructions

1. **Rinse the rice** — Place the rice in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse under cool water until the
   water runs mostly clear. Drain well.
2. **Bring to a boil** — Add the rice, water, and salt to a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil over
   medium-high heat.
3. **Cover and cook (12–15 min)** — Stir once, reduce the heat to low, and cover tightly. Cook
   without lifting the lid until the water is absorbed.
4. **Rest (10 min)** — Remove the covered saucepan from the heat and let it stand for 10 minutes.
5. **Fluff and finish** — Fluff gently with a fork. Fold in the butter and sliced green onions, if
   using, and serve.

## Chef's Tips

- **Keep the lid closed** while cooking and resting; trapped steam finishes the rice evenly.
- If your rice package specifies a different water ratio, follow the package because brands can
  vary.
- Refrigerate leftovers promptly in a shallow container for up to 4 days. Reheat to 165°F with a
  splash of water.

## Goes Well With

- Maple Soy Air Fryer Chicken Thighs
- Stir-fries, teriyaki dishes, curries, and saucy braises

## Dietary Notes

Omit optional butter for a vegan, dairy-free version; butter adds milk. Check ingredient labels and
cross-contact information for allergies.

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling boil
when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

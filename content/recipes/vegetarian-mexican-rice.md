Toast long-grain rice before a covered simmer in vegetable broth, then rest it off the heat before
fluffing.

## Ingredients

### Rice

- 1.5 cups long-grain white rice
- Water for rinsing
- 3 tbsp oil, or a total of 3 tbsp oil and butter
- 1/4 cup finely diced onion
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste, or 1/3 cup tomato sauce
- 2.5 cups vegetable broth
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin

### Optional additions and serving

- 1 small jalapeño, diced
- A handful of frozen peas and carrots
- Chopped cilantro
- 1 tsp vegetarian tomato bouillon, or a pinch of smoked paprika
- 1 can black beans, drained
- Avocado and lime for serving, optional

## Instructions

1. **Drain the rinsed rice.** Rinse in a strainer under cool water and let excess water drain away.
   Heat the measured oil or oil-butter mixture in a lidded pot over medium heat.
2. **Toast before adding liquid.** Stir the rice in the fat for 4-6 minutes, until some grains take
   on a light golden color. Add onion and optional jalapeño for 2 minutes. Stir in garlic and tomato
   paste or sauce for another 30-60 seconds.
3. **Simmer covered.** Add broth, salt, cumin and optional bouillon or paprika. Bring to a boil,
   stir once, cover and lower the heat. Cook for 18-20 minutes without repeatedly lifting the lid.
4. **Prepare add-ins separately.** If using frozen vegetables, cook them according to their package
   rather than relying only on the rice's residual heat. Warm the drained black beans if using.
5. **Rest and combine.** Remove the covered pot from the burner for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork,
   then fold in the hot vegetables, beans and cilantro. Add avocado and lime when serving if
   desired.

## Notes

- The source record names Jordan in a Chef channel and describes a Rick Bayless-inspired technique;
  this is not represented as a verified Rick Bayless recipe.
- Choose vegetable broth and confirm that tomato bouillon is meat-free; a tomato label alone does
  not establish that. Use oil without butter for the vegan version.
- Rice texture depends on grain type and the pot lid. The recorded 2.5 cups broth is retained;
  consult the rice package if using a materially different rice.
- Check packaged broth and bouillon for wheat, milk and other allergens.

## Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in shallow covered containers at 40°F or below within 2 hours of cooking or
preparation, or within 1 hour when the surroundings exceed 90°F. Use within 3-4 days. Reheat to
165°F throughout, adding a little water or the recipe liquid if needed; use a thermometer rather
than time alone. Freeze cooled rice in shallow meal-sized portions; thaw in the refrigerator or
reheat safely from frozen.

[USDA cooling, storage and reheating guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

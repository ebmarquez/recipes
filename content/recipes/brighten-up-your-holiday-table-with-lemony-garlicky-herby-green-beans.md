Briefly blanched beans get a warm garlic-butter finish and a separate lemon-scented breadcrumb
topping.

## Ingredients

- 12 oz green beans, stem ends trimmed
- 12 cups water for blanching
- 1/2 cup kosher salt for blanching, plus 1/2 tsp for the skillet
- Ice and cold water for cooling
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 3 tbsp panko breadcrumbs
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh sage
- Flaky sea salt, to finish if needed

## Instructions

1. **Cook and chill the beans.** Bring the 12 cups water and 1/2 cup kosher salt to a boil. Prepare
   a bowl of ice water nearby. Add the beans for 30-45 seconds, then transfer them to the cold
   water. Drain thoroughly and pat dry; discard the blanching water.
2. **Make the topping.** In a small skillet over medium heat, stir the panko in 1 tbsp oil for 3-4
   minutes, until lightly browned. Transfer to a dish and mix in the lemon zest.
3. **Reheat without scorching.** Warm the other tablespoon of oil in a large skillet over
   medium-high heat, stopping short of smoke. Add the drained beans, the remaining 1/2 tsp kosher
   salt, garlic and butter. Toss for about a minute; continue only as needed to warm the beans and
   soften the garlic.
4. **Finish at the table.** Turn off the heat and stir in the lemon juice and sage. Add the crumbs
   immediately before serving. Taste before adding flaky salt.

## Notes

- This makes six small side portions from 12 oz of beans.
- The large first measure of salt seasons water that is discarded; do not put the full 1/2 cup into
  the finished vegetables.
- Keep the crumbs separate until serving so they stay dry. Refrigerate blanched beans if they are
  not going directly to the skillet.
- Panko contains wheat, and butter contains milk. Check product labels and cross-contact information
  for allergies.

## Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in shallow covered containers at 40°F or below within 2 hours of cooking or
preparation, or within 1 hour when the surroundings exceed 90°F. Use within 3-4 days. Reheat to
165°F throughout, adding a little water or the recipe liquid if needed; use a thermometer rather
than time alone. Freezing the finished dish is not recommended for texture; the crumbs lose their
crispness.

[USDA cooling, storage and reheating guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety).

Crispy-edged soy chorizo browned with tender potato and onion, rolled in corn tortillas and
smothered in red sauce. The chorizo does the heavy lifting — smoky, spiced, and meaty enough that it
eats like the real thing. Allow time for cooking the filling and baking the assembled enchiladas.

**Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** About 36-45 minutes, plus assembly and oven heating | **Total:** At
least 46-55 minutes, plus assembly | **Yield:** 4-6 servings

## Ingredients

- 8-10 corn tortillas
- 2 cups shredded Mexican blend cheese (Monterey Jack + cheddar)
- 1 can (15oz) enchilada sauce _or_ homemade (see below)
- 1 package (12oz) soy chorizo (Trader Joe's or Cacique Soyrizo)
- 2 cups potato, diced small (½") — about 2 medium russet or Yukon
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can (15oz) black beans, drained and rinsed (optional, bulks it up)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Additional oil for greasing the baking dish or warming tortillas, as needed
- Water for boiling potatoes, as needed
- ½ tsp cumin, salt to taste
- **Toppings:** crema/sour cream, cotija or queso fresco, cilantro, diced avocado, pickled
  jalapeños, lime wedges

### Quick Homemade Sauce

- 2 tbsp butter (or olive oil to keep it vegan)
- 2 tbsp flour
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 can (8oz) tomato sauce

Melt the sauce butter (or warm the listed oil alternative, if using) in a saucepan over medium heat.
Whisk in the flour, chili powder, cumin and garlic powder. Gradually whisk in the broth and tomato
sauce; bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes, stirring frequently.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Boil or microwave the diced potato until just fork-tender (~6-8 min),
   then drain. (Microwave: cover, 4-5 min.)
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Sauté onion and garlic until softened, 3-4 min.
3. Squeeze the soy chorizo out of its casing into the pan and break it up. Cook 5-6 min, letting it
   crisp and darken — don't stir constantly, you want browned edges.
4. Fold in the cooked potato (and black beans if using), cumin, and salt. Cook 2 more min so flavors
   marry. Stir in half the cheese off heat.
5. Warm corn tortillas so they're pliable — microwave in a damp towel, or a quick 10-second dip in
   hot oil.
6. Roll filling into tortillas, line seam-side down in a greased baking dish.
7. Cover with enchilada sauce and remaining cheese.
8. Bake at 375°F for 20-25 min until bubbly and golden.
9. Top with crema, crumbled cotija, cilantro, avocado, pickled jalapeños, and a squeeze of lime.

## Chef's Tips

- **Let the chorizo crisp** — soy chorizo is wet out of the package. High heat and patience give you
  those browned, almost-crunchy bits that carry the dish.
- **Potato sizing matters** — dice small (½") so it cooks fast and rolls easily.
- **Soyrizo brands:** Trader Joe's Soy Chorizo and Cacique Soyrizo are both excellent and widely
  available. El Burrito Soyrizo is the classic.
- **Vegan swap:** use oil instead of butter in the sauce and dairy-free shredded cheese; skip cotija
  or queso fresco, and use dairy-free crema or omit crema.
- **Meal prep:** The chorizo-potato filling keeps 4 days in the fridge and is a killer breakfast
  taco / burrito filling with a fried egg. Assemble the full dish ahead, cover with foil, and bake
  next day — allow at least 5-10 extra minutes and confirm the center reaches 165°F.

## Dietary Notes

For a vegan version use oil, dairy-free cheese and dairy-free crema or omit crema, and skip
cotija/queso fresco. Check soy chorizo, tortillas and purchased sauce. Check ingredient labels and
cross-contact information for allergies.

## Safe Handling

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers at 40°F or below, within 2 hours of preparation (1 hour
above 90°F). Use within 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165°F; bring soups and sauces to a rolling boil
when reheating. Thaw frozen portions in the refrigerator, not on the counter.
